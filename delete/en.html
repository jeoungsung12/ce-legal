Account deletion

Count Eleven

In the game

  1. Open the game and go to Menu > Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Your account, nickname, cloud-saved progress and records, and the game files on your device are deleted immediately. If you signed in with Apple, the Sign in with Apple link is revoked. Deleted accounts cannot be restored.

If you cannot use the app

Email onsaemigol@gmail.com with "Account deletion request", the sign-in method you used (Apple/Google) and your in-game nickname. After verifying it is you, we delete the account within 10 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: account identifier, email, nickname, progress, training and mastery, fight records, settings.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Error logs, which cannot identify you, are deleted after 90 days.

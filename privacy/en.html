Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Applies to the mobile game "Count Eleven" ("the game")

onsaemigol (an independent developer, "we") processes players' personal information as described below, in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

1. Purposes

  • Accounts and sign-in: Guests get an anonymous account identifier so their progress can be saved. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we identify you so you can continue on another device. Sparring with a friend requires an Apple or Google account.
  • Saving progress: story progress, training and mastery, fight records (your personal best) and settings.
  • Sparring with a friend: finding a room by its sparring code and connecting two players in real time.
  • Ranked tiers and rankings: rating and tier from your bouts with friends or random opponents, shown on the ranking screen and on the opponent card before a bout.
  • Stability: analysing errors and crashes.
  • Ads: rewarded ads shown only when you choose to watch them. There are no ads during a fight.

We do not collect national ID numbers, payment information, location, contacts, photos or sensitive information.

Your bout record (rating, win–loss record and nickname) is visible to other players. Other players can see your nickname, rating, tier, record and place on the ranking screen and on the opponent card. Your email address and sign-in account details are never shown.

During friend sparring the two devices may connect directly to reduce latency, in which case your IP address may be shared with the other player's device. Otherwise the connection is relayed through Epic Online Services.

3. Retention

  • Account, profile and game data are kept until you delete your account and destroyed immediately upon request. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days.
  • When you delete your account, your ranking entry (nickname, rating, record) is deleted first. Per-bout result and settlement records are kept to prevent fixed bouts; they hold only an account identifier and ratings, not your nickname.
  • Error logs are deleted after 90 days.
  • Information that must be kept by law is stored separately for the required period.

4. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties, except where required by law or requested by investigative authorities under legal procedures.

6. International transfers

These processors handle information on servers outside Korea (United States and elsewhere). Information is transferred over the network when you use the related feature. See each processor's policy: Firebase, Epic Games, Unity. If you do not want these transfers, you can delete your account; ad-related transfers can be reduced by not watching ads or limiting ad tracking in your device settings.

7. Apple and Google

You authenticate directly on Apple's or Google's screen. We never receive your password. Apple and Google process their own account information under their own policies.

8. Destruction

Information is destroyed without delay when the retention period ends or when you delete your account. Game files on your device are deleted together by the in-app account deletion feature.

9. Your rights

  • You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing at any time.
  • You can change your nickname and delete your account in the game under Menu > Account. If you cannot use the app, see Account deletion.
  • Send other requests to the contact in section 13; we will respond within 10 days.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and database security rules so that only you can access your own data. Server secrets are kept out of source code, and access to personal information is limited.

11. Identifiers and opting out

  • The game does not use web cookies. It uses an app instance identifier (crash reporting) and an advertising identifier (ads).
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track before the first ad. If you decline, you can still play and watch rewarded ads, which will not be personalised. Change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.

12. Children

The game is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it immediately.

13. Contact

14. Changes

This policy applies from November 13, 2026 (bout records added November 21, 2026 — draft). We will announce changes in the game and on this page 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes unfavourable to you).
